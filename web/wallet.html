How to cash out

Your dollars are USDC on the Arc network. To turn them into money in your bank:

  1. Open an exchange account that supports USDC on Arc, for example Kraken or KuCoin.
  2. In the exchange, choose Deposit → USDC and pick Arc as the network. Copy the deposit address it shows.
  3. Send your USDC to that address from this page.
  4. Sell it or withdraw it to your bank from the exchange.

Always choose the Arc network when depositing. Money sent on the wrong network can be lost.

Exchange support changes over time; check your exchange's deposit page before sending.

We never ask for passwords or recovery phrases. Only you can approve a payment.
