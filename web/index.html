How it works

Three steps, two minutes

1

Choose amount and people

Say 30 drops of $10 for a hackathon. Links are created on your device, never on our servers.

2

Fund from your wallet

The money waits in an open-source contract on Arc. Anything unclaimed comes back to you.

3

Print or share the codes

People scan, sign in with email or their own wallet, and the dollars are theirs in seconds.

Why Arc

The fee is paid from the gift itself

On most blockchains, someone who receives dollars must first buy a second token to pay fees. Arc charges fees in dollars, so Dollar Drop takes a fraction of a cent from each drop, and the recipient never needs anything else.

Copy-proof claims

Every claim is signed for one recipient. A copied claim can't redirect the money.

Cancel anytime

Pause a campaign or refund every unclaimed drop from your dashboard, in one click.

Real dollars, ready to use

Recipients hold USDC they can send on or cash out at exchanges that support Arc.
