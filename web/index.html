How it works

Three steps, two minutes

1

Choose amount and people

Say 30 drops of $10 for a hackathon. Links are created on your device, never on our servers.

2

Fund from your wallet or exchange

Pay from a browser wallet, or straight from your exchange. The money waits in an open-source contract on Arc, and anything unclaimed comes back to you.

3

Print the codes or send the links

People scan or tap, sign in with email or their own wallet, and the dollars are theirs in seconds.

Paying from an exchange

Your USDC is on Kraken or KuCoin? That works too.

You don't need a crypto wallet app. Dollar Drop gives you a one-time deposit address, you withdraw to it from your exchange, and your drops are funded automatically.

1

Set up your drops

On Create drops, choose Fund from an exchange, then pick the amount and how many people.

2

Save your backup file

It downloads to your Downloads folder as dollar-drop-…-KEEP-PRIVATE.csv and holds your links plus the key to your funding wallet, the one-time address your money goes to. Keep it private, like a password.

3

Withdraw from your exchange

Send USDC to the address shown, on the Arc network. The page shows the exact amount: your drops plus a few cents of fees.

4

Drops fund themselves

As soon as the money arrives, your drops go live. Keep the page open until then.

Getting money back to your exchange

Refunds of unclaimed drops, and any unused fee money, return to your funding wallet. To manage them, open the Dashboard and choose Open with my backup file. The file is read only in your browser, never uploaded. From there you can pause the campaign, refund unclaimed drops, and send the money back to your exchange's USDC deposit address on Arc.

Your backup file is the only key to an exchange-funded campaign. If you lose it, people can still claim their drops, but you can't refund unclaimed ones or get leftover money back.
Why Arc

The fee is paid from the gift itself

On most blockchains, someone who receives dollars must first buy a second token to pay fees. Arc charges fees in dollars, so Dollar Drop takes a fraction of a cent from each drop, and the recipient never needs anything else.

Copy-proof claims

Every claim is signed for one recipient. A copied claim can't redirect the money.

Cancel anytime

Pause a campaign or refund every unclaimed drop from your dashboard, in one click.

Real dollars, ready to use

Recipients hold USDC they can send on or cash out at exchanges that support Arc.
